## What we learned / changed
- Large text breaks layouts in ways a normal laptop never shows. The
  audit found the problems; we now run it after every change.
- Two older automated tests only failed late at night: one counted "70
  minutes from now" past midnight, and one depended on the order things
  loaded after a reset. Both were real app bugs, and both are fixed in
  the app, not by changing the tests.
