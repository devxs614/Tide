## What we learned / changed
- The axolotl's missing gills and the squashed sand dollar had the same
  cause: one line of the Shore's own styling that made every shape turn
  around its own middle. The first guess (a browser quirk) was wrong; the
  second drawing showed the pattern. We corrected the notes instead of
  leaving the wrong reason in them.
- Closing a sheet and jumping to another screen at the same moment made
  the Back button fight with the app's navigation. The fix was to never
  move through history on our own, only when someone presses Back.
- Every new screen and sheet now gets its own state in the layout audit,
  so large text is checked as we build, not afterwards.
