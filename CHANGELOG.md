# Changelog

What changed in Tide, in plain words. The code is generated by Claude Code
from the team's specs; details per phase are in `docs/AI_DEV_LOG.md`.

## v2 (in progress, branch `v2-experience`)

### V10: The demo
- A Demo button (`/demo`, the menu, Settings → Demo, and the welcome
  screen): a guided tour with captions, a spotlight and a soft cursor, in
  its own workspace with example data. Your own data is never touched.
- Full tour (about 4 minutes), a 100-second pitch cut, and presenter mode
  for a clicker. Works offline.
- A safe preview of the crisis screen, only inside the demo.
- Changed from v1: demo mode no longer replaces what's on the device.

### V9: Settings 2.0
- Settings is now an index with search ("dark mode", "pet", "font"),
  quick presets (Calm, Low sensory, Focused, Playful, High contrast) that
  show what they change and can be undone, and one page per topic with a
  live preview and "Reset this section".
- Many new settings, each doing something real: the clock and date style,
  how a check-in starts and which feeling words it offers, Day Mode
  budgets, morning and evening cards, focus length and keeping the screen
  on, pattern evidence, shore labels, page transitions and more.
- Background sounds (waves, rain, brown noise) now actually play during
  focus and winding down, when sound is on.
- Export and import your settings; import a full backup, checked first,
  all or nothing.

### V8: Close the day
- A short evening wind-down with your companion (about 3 minutes, every
  step skippable): your day as a tide, today's wins, what helped and what
  was hard, what to do with loose ends, one thing to start tomorrow with
  its first step ready, a note in a bottle, and a kind summary.
- The summary comes from a new AI suggestion that only gets counts,
  titles and the words you picked, never your own notes, and never judges
  the day. Without AI, a plain template says the same facts.
- Today shows an evening card near your closing time and, the next
  morning, "Your one thing today" with its first step.
- Tasks you let go of wait in a Someday list on the board, ready to bring
  back. Patterns can now notice which strategies go with days that end
  okay, and shows what's been hard lately.
- Companion lines no longer show an error when a voice can't play.

### V7: Your Shore
- The Shore tab: every win washes up on your own beach (shells by kind of
  task, a pebble when you got unstuck, sea glass for a switch, a starfish
  for a strategy that helped, a sand dollar for your own wins), firsts are
  pearls, and new things wash in when you visit. Today, this week or all.
- The sky follows the time of day; at night your companion sleeps.
- Tap anything to remember what it was. Everything works with the
  keyboard, and at motion Off it's a still picture.
- Messages in a bottle: write a kind note to future you; on a hard day,
  a day or more later, it washes up on Today. Keep it, or let it drift.
- A Collection page (a field guide, not a checklist), "Save as image",
  and a little strip of your shore on Today.
- Your data now includes notes and wrap-ups in exports and in "Delete
  everything".

### V6: Onboarding 2.0 and What's new
- A new twelve-step setup that feels like making a profile, with no
  account: your name, your companion (name, color, accessory), a senses
  preset, colors, how Tide talks, read-aloud, your day, privacy, someone
  you trust, and a coached first check-in that ends with the first shell
  on your shore. Every step can be skipped, and "Skip setup" finishes in
  one tap. Closing the app resumes where you were.
- People who used v1 see "What's new" once (and any time from the menu)
  and keep their current look until they choose. Today asks "Choose a
  companion?" until they pick one or say no thanks.
- "Warn me before switching activities" now sets how early the Bridge
  appears (5, 10 or 15 minutes).
- Fixed: typing fast in a name field could drop letters.

### V5: Celebrations
- Wins are celebrated in a way that fits your day: sparkles and (for big
  moments) confetti when you feel good, a soft glow and "That counted." on
  a low day, one slow ripple and "Done. One less thing." when a lot is
  going on. Never an overlay on a hard day.
- Big moments (getting unstuck, a hard task done) get a short overlay with
  your companion and a Continue button; at most one every 10 minutes.
- Firsts are celebrated once: first check-in, focus block, strategy,
  switch and small first step.
- Nothing celebrates during breathing, while typing, or on the support
  pages. Motion Off turns movement into words.
- Pages glide in, Focus and the Companion Studio slide up, and feeling
  words arrive one after another.

### V4: Companions across the app
- Today: the companion by the greeting says hello once per part of the day
  and opens the Companion Studio when tapped; other tabs show its head in
  the top bar.
- Check-in, Unstick and the Bridge: the companion keeps you company at the
  top of the sheet, listens while you type and reacts kindly when you save.
- Focus: body doubling beside the water. Box breathing: the companion
  breathes with you in the square. Small company in other exercises.
- Friendly error and "page drifted away" pages, designed empty states, and
  a quiet splash while Tide loads.
- Never on the crisis screen. Anyone who hasn't chosen a companion yet sees
  the app exactly as before.

### V3: Companions
- Six original companions (Pebble the otter, Lolo the axolotl, Capi the
  capybara, Zip the gecko, Luma the jellyfish, Shelby the hermit crab), or
  none. Each has 14 expressions, four colors and 12 accessories, all
  unlocked from the start.
- They breathe, blink, react and have a signature move; only one animates
  at a time, and they hold still off screen or at motion Off.
- Kind lines for every moment, in each companion's voice (Gentle) or plain
  shared lines (Direct, Literal). No guilt, no "missed you".
- The Companion Studio (`/companion`, also from Settings → Companion):
  name, look, how much they talk, where they appear, their voice, a
  one-breath preview, and Undo for species and name changes.

### V2: Sound, haptics and voice
- Soft synthesized sounds (off by default): taps, switches, chimes, shore
  sounds and companion chirps, plus waves, rain or brown noise for focus.
  Settings → Sound & haptics, with a sample.
- Gentle vibration where the phone supports it.
- A new read-aloud engine: the most natural voice on the device, text
  rewritten for speech, long text that never gets cut off, and optional
  natural cloud voices (OpenAI or ElevenLabs) if the team adds a key.
- Settings → Voice: hear each voice before choosing, speed, accent, and
  switches for reading AI suggestions, companion lines and breathing cues.
- Box breathing can speak "Breathe in… Hold… Breathe out".
- The crisis screen and support page never play sounds.

### V1: Design system 2.0 and themes
- A new look: chunky cards with a solid ledge, buttons that press down,
  rounded Nunito headings, a five-tab bar with a Shore tab, and a 3D
  Check in button.
- 15 themes (Settings → Appearance), all tested for readable contrast, plus
  "follow my device" with separate light and dark picks.
- Fonts: Playful (Nunito + Atkinson), Hyperlegible, Lexend or System.
- Motion levels: Off, Calm, Lively, or match the device.
- High-contrast themes and the color-blind palette show shapes instead of
  colored dots.
- Existing users keep exactly their v1 look until they choose something new.

### V0: Baseline and safety net
- Tagged the last v1 state as `v1-baseline`.
- `npm run test:v1` runs the v1 tests on their own, so a v2 change that
  breaks a v1 behavior shows up clearly.
- Dictation is frozen: a test fails if anyone changes it.
- `docs/V1_INVENTORY.md` maps every v1 route, component, setting, table and
  test, so nothing disappears unnoticed.
- `scripts/measure-first-load.mjs` measures the JavaScript each page loads.

## v1 (2026-10-29)

The original build from `docs/TIDE_BUILD_PROMPT.md`: check-in with AI
words, Day Modes, tasks with Unstick and focus blocks, the Bridge between
activities, the toolkit, patterns with evidence, the proof log, onboarding,
settings, support and the crisis screen, and demo mode. After Phase 10:
clearer and gentler read-aloud, numbers read in English, a read-aloud voice
picker, and a fix for duplicate keys in strategy steps.
